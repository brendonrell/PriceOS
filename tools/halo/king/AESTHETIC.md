# AESTHETIC.md — Halo Collection Aesthetic Bible

The brief for every coder building the flagship generative collection. Read it
all. The mandate: **BRIGHT cyberpunk, "think anime."** Truly abstract. Painterly,
hazy, busy, varied. The ground is ALWAYS bright or richly colored — **never a
black void with neon on top.** That cliché is dead on arrival here.

North star in one line: *Promare's candy color blocks + Heike Story's brushed
paper texture + holographic foil glints, composed like a real abstract painting —
no cities, no nature, no centered blob.*

---

## 1. PALETTES (the law — ground/field is bright, never near-black)

Each palette names roles: **ground/field** (the dominant atmospheric base — must
read as a color, not darkness), **mid** (the body masses), **accent** (the
electric pops), **highlight** (the bloom/foil sparkle). Pull these exact hexes or
interpolate within the family.

**1. PROMARE BURN** — candy pink/cyan flat-block joy, the house default.
- ground `#ff5ec7` · field `#5ce1e6` · mid `#b967ff` · accent `#fffb96` · highlight `#ffffff`
- Hot magenta against pool-cyan, sherbet yellow flares. Pure Galo energy.

**2. ACID BLOOM** — acid lime + magenta, aggressive and bright.
- ground `#c2ff3d` · field `#ff2bd6` · mid `#39ff85` · accent `#7c4dff` · highlight `#f6ffd6`
- Toxic-sweet. Lime ground that buzzes, magenta shear cutting through.

**3. TANGERINE DUSK** — tangerine + violet, warm-to-cool sunset without a sunset.
- ground `#ff8a3d` · field `#a05cff` · mid `#ff5ea8` · accent `#ffe14d` · highlight `#fff0d6`
- Orange field melting into violet haze. Heat with depth.

**4. MINT CORAL** — mint + coral, soft-bright and breathable.
- ground `#8df0c8` · field `#ff7a85` · mid `#5cc8ff` · accent `#ffd166` · highlight `#fdfbe9`
- Gelato palette. Calmer ground, lets texture do the talking.

**5. ELECTRIC GOLD** — electric blue + gold, rich and regal.
- ground `#2b8cff` · field `#ffd23f` · mid `#00e5d0` · accent `#ff4fa3` · highlight `#fff7d6`
- Cobalt field with gilt foil. Reads expensive, never dark.

**6. EDGERUNNER BRIGHT** — Edgerunners neon, but on a saturated ground not a void.
- ground `#ff2e88` · field `#00e0ff` · mid `#8a4dff` · accent `#caff39` · highlight `#ffffff`
- The series' green/cyan/magenta, re-grounded on hot pink so it never goes terminal-black.

**7. HOLO FOIL** — iridescent pearl shift, the foil-fold palette.
- ground `#e19bd9` · field `#84b5e5` · mid `#a1d5b4` · accent `#efcaa7` · highlight `#f3ecff`
- Soft holographic sheen (from the Holographic Mode set). Milky but luminous — use for the iridescent-fold motifs.

**8. PEACHY HOLOGRAM** — warm holographic, peach through aqua to lavender.
- ground `#80e8dd` · field `#f9c1a0` · mid `#af81e4` · accent `#e784ba` · highlight `#b7f6af`
- Pearly and warm. The "happy" holo, good for hazy bloom fields.

**9. FUCHSIA STORM** — saturated vaporwave, deep but not dark.
- ground `#e93479` · field `#153cb4` · mid `#f62e97` · accent `#f9ac53` · highlight `#ffd9ec`
- Valentina pink over impact blue, ember flares. Dense and dramatic; floor stays vivid.

**10. CYBER SORBET** — the classic bright-vaporwave five, candy and clean.
- ground `#ff71ce` · field `#01cdfe` · mid `#05ffa1` · accent `#b967ff` · highlight `#fffb96`
- The reference vaporwave-bright set. Use when you want unmistakable "this is the vibe."

**11. HEIKE SILK BRIGHT** — Heike Story earthen palette, saturated up to anime-bright.
- ground `#ffcf4d` · field `#3f7d6e` · mid `#3a5bd9` · accent `#ff6f5e` · highlight `#fff4d6`
- Saffron/moss/indigo brushed-silk feel, pushed bright. Best for the painterly/watercolor-edge work.

**12. PRISM SPECTRUM** — full-spectrum refraction, every accent is a different hue.
- ground `#ff7ad9` · field `#7af0ff` · mid `#b6ff6e` · accent `#ffb14d` · accent2 `#8c7bff` · highlight `#ffffff`
- Rainbow shard energy. Use for prismatic-shatter and interference pieces where many hues coexist.

> Cross-palette rule: pick ONE ground per piece and let it dominate ~40–55% of
> the canvas as atmosphere. The other roles ride on top. If your ground ever
> drops below ~25% lightness, you've drifted to the void — pull it back up.

## 2. COMPOSITION RULES (kill the centered blob)

Do-list. Every piece must satisfy most of these.

- **Off-center anchor on a thirds intersection.** Place the primary focal mass at
  one of the four rule-of-thirds points, never dead center. Let it sit ~1/3 in
  from an edge.
- **Asymmetric balance, not symmetry.** Counterweight a big heavy mass on one
  side with a small dense/high-contrast incident on the other — a tiny bright
  foil glint can balance a large soft field. Unequal weights, settled tension.
- **Texture-vs-calm trade.** Balance a heavily worked/busy region against a clean,
  flat color area opposite it. The eye travels between them instead of parking.
- **Active negative space.** Reserve 25–40% as breathing field. Negative space is
  composition, not emptiness — shape it deliberately and let it carry color/haze.
- **Full-bleed diagonals.** Run primary energy (flows, shear, bands) edge-to-edge
  on a diagonal so nothing feels boxed or self-contained. No motif fully framed
  inside the canvas with margin all around.
- **Layered depth (3+ planes).** Build a hazy far field, a mid mass layer, and a
  crisp near layer (sharp shard / foil fleck / grain). Depth via blur + scale +
  opacity falloff, not perspective.
- **Lead-in and exit.** Give the eye an entry edge and a path; let at least one
  element run off-canvas so the world feels larger than the frame.
- **One focal hierarchy.** One dominant, one or two secondary, the rest support.
  Never two co-equal centers fighting.
- **Varied formats — bake aspect into the generator.** Don't ship all squares.
  Mix: **portrait 4:5 and 2:3**, **landscape 3:2 and 16:9**, **square 1:1** as
  the minority. Compose differently per format — portraits want vertical flow,
  landscapes want sweeping diagonals.

## 3. ABSTRACT MOTIF VOCABULARY (bright + tech, never cities/nature)

Build the visual language from these. Combine 2–3 per piece, not all.

- **Prismatic shards** — angular glass/crystal planes refracting the palette,
  each facet a different hue split; scatter off-center, varied scale.
- **Iridescent foil folds** — crumpled holographic sheet with soft pearl hue-shift
  along the creases; use HOLO FOIL / PEACHY HOLOGRAM palettes.
- **Fluid color masses** — large soft flowing pigment bodies (think poured ink in
  water, but bright), edges bleeding, no hard outline.
- **Interference bands / moiré** — overlapping fine line grids that beat into soft
  rainbow ripples; keep them as a textured region, not the whole frame.
- **Datamosh shear planes** — horizontal/diagonal smear blocks where color "melts"
  and ghosts across a boundary; a TECH accent, used sparingly as incident.
- **Pixel-sort streaks** — vertical/horizontal runs of sorted color reading as
  luminous combed silk; great as a directional texture band.
- **Light-leak blooms** — over-exposed soft glows leaking from an edge, washing
  the palette warmer/brighter where they land.
- **Holographic gradients** — smooth multi-stop pearlescent sweeps used as a
  field or as the ground itself; the anti-void base layer.
- **Glitch shear / RGB split** — controlled chromatic offset and block displacement
  as a fine accent at focal edges — flecks, not the subject.
- **Energy filaments / flow lines** — flow-field particle trails (Hobbs-style)
  sweeping a diagonal, bright on a bright ground, suggesting motion.
- **Glyph dust / HUD flecks** — faint scattered tech-glyph particles and prismatic
  HUD specks as far-layer texture only; whisper level, never legible "neon text."
- **Refraction caustics** — rippling bright light-bands as if through cut glass,
  laid over a color field to add shimmer and incident.

## 4. TEXTURE TECHNIQUES (painterly, hazy, anime — never muddy)

- **Watercolor bleed edges.** Soft feathered boundaries where two color masses
  meet, with bloom halos (the wet-on-wet effect). Avoid hard vector edges on the
  big forms.
- **Paper/vellum grain.** Overlay a subtle paper tooth (Heike Story's brushed-
  parchment finish) — multiply at low opacity to soften gradients without dulling.
- **Flat-block + outline accents.** Borrow Promare: some forms get bold flat color
  with a confident dark-but-colored contour (not black) for graphic punch.
- **Bloom / soft glow.** Bright cores spill light into neighbors; lift highlights
  toward white, keep the spill colored. This is the "joyful" lighting.
- **Chromatic bleed.** Gentle RGB separation at high-contrast edges for a
  holographic shimmer — restrained, a few px, focal areas only.
- **Layered grain over haze.** Fine luminance grain on top of soft gradients keeps
  big hazy fields alive and filmic instead of flat or banded.
- **Kuwahara/painterly smoothing.** Run a painterly filter pass so detail clumps
  into brushy strokes rather than photographic noise — "living painting" finish.
- **Anti-mud guard.** Never let complementary masses blend to grey. Separate them
  with a bright transition hue or a clean negative-space gap. Saturation stays up.

## 5. HARD ANTI-PATTERNS (do NOT do — instant reject)

- ❌ Black / near-black / charcoal grounds or voids. The ground is a COLOR.
- ❌ Neon lines or "text" glowing on a dark background (the dead cliché).
- ❌ A single object centered in a square. No dead-center symmetry.
- ❌ Literal cityscapes, skylines, buildings, streets, neon signage.
- ❌ Literal nature: leaves, trees, water, mountains, clouds, flowers, sky.
- ❌ Representational anything — no figures, faces, objects, logos.
- ❌ Muddy desaturation, greyed-out blends, washed-dull palettes.
- ❌ Symmetric, mirror-balanced, evenly-spaced "wallpaper" layouts.
- ❌ One uniform texture edge-to-edge with no focal hierarchy or negative space.
- ❌ All pieces the same aspect ratio.

## 6. NAMED REFERENCES (aim your mind's eye here)

- **Promare** (Studio Trigger) — flat candy color blocks, neon pink/cyan flame,
  joyful saturation, bold contour. The color north star.
- **Cyberpunk: Edgerunners** — high-sat neon hue language (re-grounded bright).
- **BNA: Brand New Animal** (Trigger) — punchy flat color, energetic motion.
- **The Heike Story** (Science SARU, Naoko Yamada) — brushed-paper texture,
  shadowless painterly fields, "living painting" finish.
- **Tyler Hobbs** (*Fidenza*) — flow-field structure, sophisticated asymmetric
  composition, confident negative space.
- **Manolo Gamboa Naon** — plastic color/geometry experimentation, chaos-vs-order
  balance, rich abstract incident.
- **Refik Anadol** (*Machine Hallucinations*) — vast hazy data-flow color masses,
  atmospheric depth, immersive bright fields.
- **Kim Asendorf** — pixel-sorting as fine art; the streak/silk texture reference.
- General touchstones: holographic foil photography, iridescent dichroic glass,
  bright vaporwave gradient art, poured-ink/alcohol-ink abstracts.
