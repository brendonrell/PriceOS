## Jury lenses
- CURATOR (art-snob / gallery): originality, taste, concept, would it hang.
- COLLECTOR (market): would I mint/own it, 256-output longevity, grail seeds.
- BRAND (PD fit): surreal real-but-off, hazy/textured, all-new vs catalog, distinct palette.
- CRAFT (execution): composition, variation across seeds, finish, no dead frames.

## Format (NHL-style, 12 entrants)
- Seed 1–12 from the panel's full-field ranking.
- Play-in: 5v12, 6v11, 7v10, 8v9 → 4 winners. (1–4 bye)
- Quarters: 1 vs W(8/9), 2 vs W(7/10), 3 vs W(6/11), 4 vs W(5/12).
- Semis, Final → champion (the HALO project).
