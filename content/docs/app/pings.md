# The App — Pings

Pings are PD's notification primitive — the platform tapping you on the shoulder. Every ping wears the same glyph its concept wears everywhere else in the app, so a notification reads at a glance.

## What fires

| Ping | Glyph | Fires when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collected | ✶ | A mint you care about happens |
| Sold | ✶ | Your piece sells |
| Listed | ✹ | A broadcast listing lands |
| Offer / Offer accepted | ✦ | An offer arrives or resolves |
| Transfer | ✸ | A watched piece moves |
| Follow | ⚭ | Someone follows you (or a Project you follow acts) |
| Wishlist hit | ✛ | A wishlisted piece gets market action |
| Watch hit | ✛ | A watched item triggers |
| Streak | ◈ | A streak advances |
| Achievement | ◍ | An unlock lands |

## What you control

The **MY PINGS** row in settings is a bank of per-kind toggles: mints ✶, lists ✹, offers ✦, transfers ✸, mutuals-only ⚭ (restrict social pings to mutual follows), ping-toasts ⇡ (surface pings as live toasts as they happen), and **night mode ⏾** (silence, on a schedule you own).

## The inbox

The Pings panel holds a rolling window of your newest hundred: directed pings (things that happened *to you*) and follow-feed pings (things that happened in your orbit) interleaved, newest first, live — activity lands in the panel within about a second of landing on-chain or in the app.

## Reaching your devices

Pings arrive three ways, escalating by how much you've let PD in:

1. **In-app** — the inbox, always.
2. **Toasts** — live surfacing while you browse, if ping-toasts is on.
3. **Native push** — installed-app notifications on supported devices (web push; on iOS, install PriceOS to the home screen and allow notifications).
