# The App — The Spell Book

The Spell Book is where PriceOS keeps its lenses — pill-toggleable modifiers that change how you see the platform rather than what's on it. Each pill flips one worldview on or off; they stack, they persist, and half the fun is discovering what a combination does to a page you thought you knew.

## How it works

The Spell Book opens from settings. Each spell is a pill wearing its glyph; active pills stay lit and their effects follow you across every page until you release them. Spells are per-user and purely presentational — nothing here changes your data or anyone else's view.

## A sampling of spells

- **Redacted @** — strips identity from the interface: browse the market without the who.
- **Price Ghost ᗝ** — the price layer goes spectral.
- **The Watch ⬬** — the monitoring layer. See [Collector Tools](/docs/app/collector-tools).
- **Stargazing ⍟** — the celestial lens; the platform's astrological substrate (every Output has a birth chart) comes forward.
- **Spite Book** — the grudge ledger. The pieces that got away, kept where you can see them.
- **Panopticon** — the all-seeing view. Deliberately confirmatory — the app asks before it lets you turn it on.
- **Mood Ring ⌬** — the daily platform colour as a lens. See [The Shell](/docs/app/the-shell).

The full set is longer and grows over time — the Spell Book is where new lenses land first. Some spells reveal themselves only in use; documenting each one exhaustively here would spoil the half of the platform that's meant to be found.

## Spells vs. display modes

Display modes ([Settings & Display](/docs/app/settings-and-display)) change how the interface *renders* — light, dark, zen, sticker mode. Spells change what the interface *foregrounds*. Modes are ergonomics; spells are worldviews.
