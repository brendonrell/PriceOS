# The Dispatch ▤

Every morning at 9AM (Montreal time), PD prints its own paper: one page covering the prior day, assembled from the real ledger. Coffee first, then yesterday on the record.

## Where to read it

- **`/dispatch`** — this morning's edition.
- **`/dispatch/2026-07-12`** — the permanent archive, one URL per publication date. Editions are printed once and never edited: a link cited today reads identically in five years.
- Entries live on the home page's news rail (the ▤ pill) and in the footer.

## The sections

- **Yesterday's Lead** — the day's single biggest story: the headline sale, the mint run, or the honest quiet.
- **By the Numbers** — mints, sales, volume, listings, offers, and the top sale, in one table.
- **From the Salon** — one observation about how the room behaved.
- **Noted** — short items: graduations, sell-outs, the busiest room, new work on the wall.
- **Tomorrow's Horizon** — what's coming: artist cooldowns ending, projects a few pieces from selling out.

Saturday and Sunday print the lighter **Weekend Edition**.

## How it's written

Every number is real — the same ledger that powers the stats and the feeds. The prose is deterministic and seeded per edition: the paper costs nothing to run and never invents a fact. Quiet days print too; the archive keeps the silence as faithfully as the noise.
