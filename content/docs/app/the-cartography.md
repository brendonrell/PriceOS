# The Cartography ◫

PD drawn as a world. Every Project is a territory with its own generated coastline, every collector an inhabitant living on the land they hold, and every market action a visible event — the whole platform, alive on one map.

## Opening it

**Long-press the "Price Discussion" name on the home page.** The name is the platform's compass: hold it and the map unfolds full-screen. (Its sibling gesture, the triple-tap, opens [The Rewind](/docs/app/the-rewind).)

## Reading the map

- **Territories** are Projects. A territory's area grows with its minted supply — hot mints visibly grow the land. Its coastline is generated from the Project's identity and never changes shape, only size; its color is the Project's own colorway.
- **Inhabitants** are collectors: one dot per wallet per territory, sized by how many pieces they hold there. The artist marks the capital with the ✺ artist glyph.
- **Continents form on their own.** Territories that share collectors drift toward each other. Neighborhoods on the map are real taste-neighborhoods.

## The live layer

The map listens to the platform in real time:

- **A mint** ripples gold at the collector's seat and the territory grows.
- **A sale** sails as a comet from the seller's dot to the buyer's, price in tow.
- **A listing** sends up a beacon on the shore.

On open, the recent ledger replays as faint echo pings, so the map is never a still image.

## Three depths

1. **Macro** — all of PD at once: shapes, names, sizes.
2. **Territory** — tap one: full coastline, inhabitants, minted count, holders, volume.
3. **Wallet** — tap an inhabitant: everything else dims while that wallet's entire empire lights up across every territory it holds, connected by arcs, with its @name and totals. Tap open sea to sail back out.

Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, and the breadcrumb in the corner steps you back up a level. Everything on the map is the real ledger — the same data that powers the stats, the feeds, and the Pings.
