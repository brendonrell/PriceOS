# Pings — Artist Push

**Artist Push** is a perk of being a PD artist: once a month, per project, you can ping **every holder** of that project. It lands in each holder's Pings inbox — and on their lock screen if they've enabled 3D Pingtoasts — signed with your @name and the ✺ artist glyph.

## For artists

The control lives in [PD Studio](/docs/studio/the-artist-dashboard), on each of your live project cards:

1. Pick a **preset** — NEW WORK, DROP LIVE, THANK YOU, MILESTONE, ON THE MARKET, or LAST CALL.
2. Tap **SEND**, then confirm — the button shows exactly how many holders you're reaching before you commit.
3. Done. The card shows when your next push unlocks.

Presets are the whole message set in v1 — they keep the channel valuable for every artist by keeping it unspammable for all of them. The platform enforces the limits server-side:

- **One push per project per 30 days.** Not per preset — per project.
- Holders who muted you are skipped automatically.
- Each holder's own settings still apply: Silent Mode and their Pingtoasts mode are always respected.

Used well — a drop announcement, a sellout thank-you, a genuine milestone — Artist Push is the highest-signal channel on PD: it reaches exactly the people who already put money behind your work.

## For collectors

You'll receive an Artist Push only from artists whose work you actually hold, at most once a month per project you hold. It reads like this in your inbox:

> ✺ **@artist** just went live — minting now · @project

Tap it and you land on the project. If an artist's pushes aren't for you, mute the artist — or flip Silent Mode when you want the whole platform quiet. There is nothing to unsubscribe from, because there is no list: sell the piece, and the channel closes itself.
