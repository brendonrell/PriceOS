# Privacy & Terms

Plain language, on purpose. This page is the whole of it — there is no longer
version in legalese somewhere else.

## Privacy

### A wallet is the entire account

PD has no personal-information layer. **No email, no name, no phone number, no
KYC** — you sign in with your Ethereum wallet ([SIWE](/docs/for-collectors/wallet-setup)),
and that address is your whole identity here. Everything PD knows about you
hangs off a public wallet address you chose to connect.

The single exception is opt-in and single-purpose: subscribing to
[the Dispatch Digest](/docs/app/the-dispatch) stores the email you give it for
exactly that delivery, with one-tap unsubscribe.

### What PD stores

The platform keeps the off-chain product working by storing, against your
wallet address:

- **Your settings and workspace** — display modes, colorway, spells, grid
  presets, notes, to-dos, wishlists, stars. [What's Private](/docs/whats-private)
  is the full list of what only you ever see.
- **Your social layer** — handle, profile, follows, pings, albums, tags, and
  the rest of what you deliberately display. [What's Public](/docs/whats-public)
  is the full list of what everyone sees.
- **Native push subscriptions** — only if you opt in to 3D Pingtoasts, and
  only so your own device can be reached; turning them off removes the
  subscription.

### What PD never does

- **No ad tracking, no analytics trackers, no third-party pixels.** The site
  does not watch you for anyone.
- **No selling or sharing of data.** There is no personal data product; the
  stored state exists to run PD for you, full stop.
- **No custody.** PD never holds your keys, your funds, or your art.

### The chain is public, forever

Everything you do on Ethereum — mints, sales, transfers, the art itself — is
**public and permanent by nature**, readable by anyone with or without PD.
That's the medium, not a policy choice. PD reads the chain; it cannot edit it,
and neither can anyone else.

## Terms

By using Price Discussion you accept the following:

- **You transact at your own risk.** On-chain transactions are irreversible.
  PD surfaces information — prices, floors, rarity, history — but nothing on
  the platform is financial advice, and floor-value readouts are honest
  estimates, not promises.
- **Wallet-to-wallet means wallet-to-wallet.** Purchases, offers, trades, and
  gnome deals move value directly between users on Ethereum. PD does the
  books; it never touches the money and cannot reverse, refund, or arbitrate
  a payment.
- **The art's terms are immutable.** A Project's on-chain terms at mint are
  its terms forever — no admin keys, no pausing, no upgrades. The
  [60-day cooldown](/docs/for-artists/how-pd-works) is enforced by the factory
  contract, not by promises.
- **The filter is a quality floor.** PD is filtered, not curated — the artist
  whitelist gates entry; it does not editorialize the catalog or endorse any
  Project as an investment.
- **Platform-ledger items live in the platform ledger.** Some PD things —
  [gnomes](/docs/gnomes/overview), stickers, achievements — exist in PD's own
  books rather than on-chain, and say so plainly where they live. They are
  real inside PD and nowhere else.
- **Don't abuse the platform.** Impersonation, exploiting bugs, and attacking
  the service or its users can cost a wallet its off-chain privileges
  (handles, pings, the social layer). What's on-chain stays yours regardless —
  PD couldn't take it if it wanted to.
- **PD is provided as-is.** The platform is young and pre-mainnet surfaces may
  change; [the docs](/docs) say so when they do.
