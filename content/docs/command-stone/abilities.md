# Command Stone — Abilities

Everything the Stone understands, in one place. Type any of these into the bubble; the Stone reads the intent, answers in a widget, and offers the one obvious action as the button at the bottom.

No syntax is required — these are the phrases at their most explicit, and the Stone accepts natural variations of each. When a line doesn't match anything below, it's a search, and search is never a dead end.

## Find anything

The real global search, inline. People, projects, pieces, traits, soundtracks, pages.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `prisms` | The project, painted big, with live floor · volume · ATH |
| `@gmoney` | The collector's dossier |
| `prisms 7` | That exact piece |
| `two yellow circles` | Visual search across every output |
| `settings` | The page itself — Enter jumps straight there |

The search power grammar works here too: `by:@artist`, `under:0.1`, `listed`, `sort:rarity`.

And PriceOS itself is searchable: type any feature's name — `grid presets`, `stargazing`, `hostile takeover` — and it appears as an app: its icon in the rounded square with its name, straight out of the numbered Feature Atlas. Tap the tile to open its directory entry.

## Ask the ledger

Plain questions, answered from the same ledger the whole platform runs on.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `prisms floor` | The live floor — and the button offers to anchor it |
| `prisms ath` · `prisms volume` | All-time high · traded volume |
| `who holds prisms 7` | The piece's current holder |
| `@gmoney spent` · `collected` · `followers` | The collector's numbers |

## Do math

Type an expression, get the answer instantly. `+ − × ÷ % ^` and parentheses.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `3*67` | `= 201` |
| `(0.4 - 0.25) * 22` | `= 3.3` |

When a result lands on a number PD cares about, the Stone says so.

## Resolve a date

Say a day in plain words; the Stone pins it, counts the days, and reads that day's slate — your scheduled items and to-dos. The button seeds a to-do aimed at that exact day.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `tomorrow` | The day, resolved, with its schedule |
| `next week tuesday` | Next week's Tuesday — not the same as `next tuesday` |
| `in 18 days` | The date 18 days out, counted for you |
| `aug 30` | That date — rolls to next year if it's passed |

## Rank the platform

Standings, computed live from the ledger. Ask for any depth up to 50; the podium wears its medals.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `highest spenders on the platform top 15` | The platform's biggest wallets by ETH spent |
| `top 15 spenders` | The same, shorter |
| `top 20 mutuals by volume spent` | Your mutuals, ranked by what they've spent |
| `top 50 followers by wallet age` | Your followers, oldest accounts first |

Metrics: `by volume spent` (the default) and `by wallet age`. Every row taps through to the profile; the button opens №1.

## The news — it speaks first

When the Stone has real reason — unread pings — it leads with them the moment it opens, before you type. One keystroke stands it down; the button marks everything read. The parked corner dot pulses only while it actually knows something. No news, no theater.

## The Moods

A mood is one castable word that sets the whole room: colorway, ambient light, and the DJ in a single cast. The same word lifts it and the colorway you were wearing comes back — switch moods mid-wear and the Stone still remembers your original. Only one mood is worn at a time.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `cozy` · `cozy mood` · `mood` | The haze rolls in, the ambient light comes on, the DJ plays from your shelf |
| `rave` · `hype` | Hot colour, ambient light, the DJ — loud on purpose |
| `monk` · `focus` | Everything off, quiet: clean light page, no ambience, music stops |
| `midnight` | Dark mode plus the dim wash; whatever is playing keeps playing |
| `golden` · `golden hour` | Sunset orange, ambient light, the DJ |
| `ocean` | Open blue and the ambient wash; whatever is playing keeps playing |
| `moods` | The whole set dealt as a card — both decks, tap to wear, tap again to lift |

**The market deck.** Collecting moods — same one-word grammar, but these set your market lens (sort + real app modes) instead of the room. One room mood and one market mood can be worn at once; your old sort comes back when the lens lifts.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `bullish` · `bull` | Degen on, everything priced — the ape posture |
| `bearish` · `bear` | The Watch on — watching from the sidelines, hands in pockets |
| `hunting` · `hunt` | The Arbitrage Map reads the spreads, everything priced — deals in sight |
| `browsing` · `window shopping` | The feed sort — strolling the galleries, no agenda |

## Etch — say it, it exists

A verb word turns the line into a creation. Nothing commits without a second touch — the plan flashes as the button first, so a mis-heard line never carves junk.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `todo: buy prisms under 0.1` | A to-do with an ETH target — the Sentinel arms it and watches server-side |
| `todo: renew ens friday p1` | A dated, prioritized to-do |
| `note on prisms #7: minter said it's his favourite` | A private note on the piece |
| `anchor fumage at 0.2` | Your personal anchor price |
| `watch voltaic` | Star the project — drives your interest pings |
| `wishlist arcology #3` | Wishlist the piece |

An etched item is the real thing: the same to-do the [Sentinel](/docs/app/collector-tools) watches, the same note the editor opens.

## Cast — names flip the settings

Spell, mode and workspace names work by their exact names. Typing `fog` casts Fog. Typing `degen` flips Degen. A workspace name wears that workspace. The [Spell Book](/docs/app/spell-book) is the vocabulary.

## It remembers, and it sees

- **Memory** — the Stone keeps its last subject. `prisms floor`, then just `ath`, `calc 0.5`, `gallery`, or `30d` — all still mean Prisms.
- **Seeing** — summoned while a piece is on your screen, the Stone adopts it. A bare `floor` or `anchor 0.5` means the thing you're looking at.

## Putting it away

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `minimize` | Parks the Stone at a corner dot — tap the dot to bring it back |
| `close` | Fully dismisses it, with a word on the way out |
