# What's Public on PD

> **Rough outline — first pass.** PD is a public-by-default social layer over a public blockchain. If it lives on-chain or on your profile, assume the whole world can see it.

## The on-chain record (public forever)
- Every mint, sale, transfer, and offer — Ethereum is a public ledger.
- Who holds what, and for how long.
- Royalties and payment splits.

## Your profile
- @handle, PriceSprite, and PriceRank / PriceScore.
- Your collection and holdings, your Showcase, and your **Albums** (rolling out as public — anyone can browse your shelves).
- Achievements, faction + oath history, your forged Sigil, and your Vault.

## Social + market
- Followers and following (the social graph).
- Listings, offers, and **Takeovers** — including the public verdict (completed / partial / withstood).
- The live tape and the activity feeds.

## Standings
- Leaderboards and **podium** finishes (top-3 across the boards).
- The Book of Conquests (the faction war record).
