# PD Studio — The Artist Dashboard

A Project's contract is frozen at deployment, but a Project is not finished
at deployment — it mints, trades, gains holders, earns royalties, and keeps
having a life. The dashboard is where that life is visible, in the detail an
artist actually wants and almost never gets.

On Price Discussion, price movement is the discussion. The dashboard is that
same thesis pointed back at the maker: your work's market activity, presented
with care, as something to read — not a sales report bolted onto an upload
form.

## Your Projects, in the accordion

The dashboard lives in the Studio's connect surface — the same menu structure
the app uses, with your Projects where a collector's notes would be. Each
Project opens from the accordion into its own live view; everything below is
per-Project, with your whole catalog one tap up.

## What you can see

Everything the platform knows about your work, from the chain and from the
app, in one place:

- **The mint, as it happens.** Supply minted and remaining, the pace of the
  mint over time, and each mint as it lands — the same live events that run
  through [the Tape](/docs/app/the-shell), filtered to your work.
- **Your holders.** Who holds your Outputs, how many, since when — the
  first-mint loyalists and the collectors arriving through the secondary
  market.
- **The market.** Live listings and offers on your Outputs, sales as they
  clear, floor and price movement over the Project's life — the
  [secondary market](/docs/for-collectors/the-secondary-market), seen from
  the artist's side of the glass.
- **Royalties.** What has flowed through your Project's
  [immutable splitter](/docs/for-artists/royalties) — earned to date and per
  sale, next to the primary proceeds that already paid you at mint.

## What you can manage

The chain is immutable; the surround is not. The dashboard is where the
off-chain layer PD keeps around every Project stays in the artist's hands —
starting with your Project's **soundtrack**, updatable whenever the work
deserves a new one, with more of the surround managed from the same place as
it grows.

## Artist pings

Pings in the Studio are artist pings: mints of your work, new listings and
offers, secondary sales, and royalty payouts arrive as
[Pings](/docs/pings/overview) — including as native push notifications on an
installed phone app. The dashboard is glanceable precisely because it can
also come to you.
