# The Awakening

Gnomes are free. Nobody buys one from PD; nobody mints one on purpose. A gnome is **won by minting the project's art** — and nobody knows exactly when.

## The shut hill

Before its hour, a project's GNOME tab shows only a closed hillside: a dark mine mouth, two lamplit eyes blinking in the dark, and a line — *something lives down here*. Nothing about the keeper leaks: not its name, not its temperament, not its look. The hill is shut.

## The hidden hour

Every project has a **waking threshold**: a mint count at which its gnome wakes. The number is seeded per project, computed only on PD's servers, and published nowhere — it genuinely cannot be known from outside. Somewhere between early in the mint and deep into it, one mint crosses the line.

**The wallet whose mint strikes the hour owns the gnome.** That's the whole rule. The crossing piece's minter is recorded as the keeper's keeper the moment the threshold is crossed, permanently, along with the piece number that struck the hour and a rarity cast from the project's real traded volume at that instant. Every mint you make in an unawakened project is a lottery ticket you were buying anyway.

The awakening is written once and never rewritten. Selling the crossing piece later changes nothing — the gnome is not attached to the piece; it was won at the hour and lives in your [gnomewallet](/docs/gnomes/the-gnomewallet) until you trade it on [gnopensea](/docs/gnomes/gnopensea).

## The keeping, on record

Once awake, the gnome's tab shows the full record under its journal: who keeps it (their PD handle), the awakening date, the piece that struck the hour, and the rarity. Everyone can see it; it is part of the project's story. The keeper's gnome-world identity — the `.gnome` name, the `6n0m3…` address, the wallet itself — never appears in the tab; that whole world lives behind the [gnomewallet](/docs/gnomes/the-gnomewallet) pill in settings.

## Respect — the keeper's ladder

Own it or not, every signed-in visitor has a standing with the keeper, climbed with real ledger facts only — pieces of the project held, unbroken held-days, and **favoured** pieces (held at least 7 days, never listed):

| Rung | Name | Earned by |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | A STRANGER | no pieces held |
| 1 | KNOWN AT THE DOOR | hold ≥1 piece |
| 2 | FRIEND OF THE HILL | ≥21 total held-days |
| 3 | FAVOURED | ≥1 favoured piece |
| 4 | KIN OF THE VEIN | ≥3 favoured pieces and ≥60 held-days |

The ladder is drawn in the tab as the gnome's own gems, with an honest line naming exactly what earns the next rung. It changes what you hear: the keeper greets strangers, holders, and its favoured people from different pools entirely — and at FAVOURED, it writes **the Appraisal**: a real, fact-only written case for your longest-held piece, in its own voice. Listing a piece breaks its favour; the keeper notices such things.
