# PriceOS Suite

The **PriceOS Suite** is PD's productivity super-app — the calendar, to-dos, workflows, and notes you already use in the connect menu, opened up into one full workspace. One app, seven faces, switched with the icons at the top.

**Opening it:** press and hold the **TO-DOS** header in the connect menu — right where it says "TO-DOS". Close with the × like any other panel.

## ‰ Today

The landing dashboard. The week rides along the top — today wearing PD's dotted ring, each day showing what's due — and beneath it: today's to-dos (complete them right there), what's up next, and the live pulse: ETH earmarked across your targets, workflows armed, and your active budget. Every tile is a door into its app.

## ▦ PriceCal

The PD calendar, full size: the month grid plus the day column. A day shows its events and every dated to-do together, and each day carries its own note. Dated to-dos with a reminder time fire real reminders — into your Pings, and to your phone if pushes are on.

## ❍ PriceTask

Your to-dos, expanded. Everything the To-Dos box can do lives here at full width:

- **Magic add** — type naturally: `buy prisms 22 under .4 fri` fills the piece, the price target, and the due date in one line. Paste a whole list and every line becomes its own to-do.
- **Targets** — a BUY to-do with an ETH target watches the real listing price and flips to **READY** when the market meets your number. The war-chest line totals what you've earmarked across open targets.
- **Priorities, labels, recurrence** — P1–P3, `#labels` you can filter by, and repeating to-dos that reschedule themselves when completed.
- **☇ Workflows** — also reachable from the ☇ key in the PriceTask header, same as always.

## ☇ PriceFlows

Workflows as their own app: iOS-Shortcuts-style automations built on the to-do rails. Pick a trigger, pick the payload, **ARM** —

- **NEXT UPLOAD** — when @artist uploads → mint ×n, cut a to-do, and notify you.
- **PRICE HIT** — when a piece dips to your number → cut a to-do and notify you.

Armed workflows wait on the real feeds; fired ones stay as records wearing **FIRED**, and you can arm again freely.

## ◊ PriceBooks

The money desk: your **Budgets** (set one, activate it, and the whole app paints what fits inside it) together with your **Portfolios** — the real one and the Shadow (paper trades) — with the full holdings tree and filters.

## ƒ PriceCalc

The money brains on call: tapping PriceCalc summons **Profit Pal** right over the Suite — your portfolio tiles (pieces · spent · at-floor · net), per-project exit paths priced on the Calc's real rate card, and the HOT read off the live tape. Close it and you're back in the Suite.

## ⊟ PriceNotes

Every note you've written, in one list — the notes you attach to pieces, artists, and calendar days. Tap any note to read or edit it in place.

Everything in the Suite is the same data as everywhere else in PD — a to-do added here is the same to-do in the connect menu, on the calendar, and in your reminders. It all follows your account across devices.
