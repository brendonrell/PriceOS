# PriceScore

Every wallet on PD carries a **PriceScore**: the sum of the points from every achievement it has unlocked. There are 1,000 achievements in the catalog, from first-day unlockables to multi-year monuments, and each one carries a fixed point value. Your score is simply what you've actually done, added up.

You'll see it on your profile's Achievements wall, in your PriceSprite modal, on the leaderboards, and folded into your identity anywhere the app introduces you.

## PriceRank — the tier your score buys

PriceScore is the number; **PriceRank** is the tier. When your score crosses a threshold you level up, RPG-style — the circled number beside your PriceSprite is your current tier.

| Tier | Title | PriceScore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ❶ | Initiate | 100 |
| ❷ | Participant | 400 |
| ❸ | Regular | 1,000 |
| ❹ | Established | 2,500 |
| ❺ | Notable | 5,000 |
| ❻ | Respected | 10,000 |
| ❼ | Authority | 20,000 |
| ❽ | Luminary | 40,000 |
| ❾ | Legend | 75,000 |
| ❿ | Apex | 120,000 |

Everyone starts at tier ⓿ — unranked. The big tiers sit in year-scale territory on purpose.

## Farming is pointless by design

Rank comes from un-fakeable, on-chain truth: primary spend, trading volume, holdings, tenure, artist activity. Free actions — stars, wishlists, albums, follows, streak taps — still unlock their achievements for the feedback, but their combined score contribution is hard-capped so low that a pure free-action grinder never reaches tier ❶. There's no policing because there's nothing to police: the only way up is a real collecting life.

## PriceStreak — the third leg

Beside score and rank lives your **PriceStreak** — consecutive days on PD. It has its own achievements (which feed your score) and its own rewards; on a [Keychain](/docs/keychains/overview), the chain literally is your streak.
