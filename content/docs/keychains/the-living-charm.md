# The Living Charm

A Keychain out of the capsule is a finished character — but a blank life. Everything that happens next is written by you.

**How:** Wear it from the Depanneur ⚷; POLISH there syncs your streak and rank onto the charm.

## The chain is your streak

Your charm hangs from the ring by whatever your PriceStreak has earned. The full ladder:

| Streak | The chain | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| under 7 days | CORD | a bare cord — everyone starts here |
| 7 days | STARTED | the first four real links |
| 30 days | LINKED | six links |
| 60 days | FORGED | eight links — the streak's activation mark |
| 100 days | HEAVY | ten links, thickened |
| 365 days | **GOLD** | twelve links — chain AND keyring recast in gold |
| 1,000 days | **CHROME** | the monument — the whole rig in chrome |

One missed day resets a PriceStreak — that has always been the rule, and the chain tells it honestly. There is no way to buy a longer chain. There is only tomorrow.

## The finish is your rank

PriceRank recasts the toy itself, tier by tier:

| PriceRank | Finish | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| tiers 0–2 | MATTE | the base cast — plastic dome or dull rubber |
| tier 3 · Regular | GLOSS | a soft light sweep across the upper body |
| tier 5 · Notable | GLITTER | a sparkle field seeded into the body — it twinkles |
| tier 7 · Authority | GOLD | the whole charm poured in gold |
| tier 9 · Legend | CHROME | liquid chrome — the reference finish |

Rank comes only from un-fakeable onchain truth — free actions can't climb it — so a chrome charm is a résumé you can dangle.

## Polish — the sync ritual, exactly

Your streak and rank live on PD; your charm lives onchain. **POLISH** joins them:

1. You ask to polish. The app writes a sealed attestation of your current PriceStreak and PriceRank — signed by PD's own settlement key, addressed to your wallet and your specific charm, numbered, and short-lived.
2. You submit it with a tap and pay the gas — pennies. PD pays nothing, ever, no matter how many people polish.
3. The charm re-draws itself with the new chain and finish, and records the moment.

The fine print that keeps it honest: each attestation works **once** (it carries a counter the contract ticks forward), **expires** if you sit on it, is **keeper-only** (nobody can polish your charm, and you can't polish one you don't own), and is **tamper-proof** — edit a single digit of the claim and the contract rejects it. Your numbers are exactly what the app signed, or nothing.

Polish whenever you like — after a milestone, before a flex, or just to keep the shine honest. Between polishes, the charm simply shows the last attested day.

## The bond

Shine cannot be bought, inherited, or transferred — only lived. The exact semantics:

| On a hand-to-hand transfer… | |
|---|---|
| chain (streak) | **resets to bare cord** |
| finish (rank) | **resets to base** |
| polish history | **cleared** |
| shape, palette, material, face, pose, accessory | survive forever |
| christened name | survives forever |

The new keeper polishes their OWN life onto it, starting immediately. This is what makes a worn charm on someone's profile mean something: it is proof of days, in an economy where days can't be faked.

## Christening

Once — and only once, ever — a keeper may name their charm:

- **2 to 12 characters**: capitals A–Z, digits 0–9, and spaces (never leading or trailing).
- The name is engraved on a **white tag hanging off the keyring**, in the house type.
- It is **permanent across all future keepers** — the one part of a charm's life that transfers. Sell MOMO and MOMO stays MOMO.

Choose like it's forever, because it is.

## Wearing it

Equip one of your Keychains and it appears as a **mini charm at the end of your profile tags** — the only place on PD your streak and rank exist as an object other people can see. Nothing is worn by default; the flex is a choice. Tap anyone's worn charm to see the full keychain, chain and all.

## Built to outlive everything

The charm's genes are frozen at the crank; the chain and finish are attested by the app and verified by the contract; the art — including the swing and the blink — is drawn by the contract itself, forever, with no server in the loop. If PD's lights went out tomorrow, every Keychain would keep rendering, keep swinging, and keep blinking — carrying the last polished day of its keeper's life, onchain, indefinitely.
