# The Living Charm

A Keychain out of the capsule is a finished character — but a blank life. Everything that happens next is written by you.

## The chain is your streak

Your charm hangs from the ring by whatever your PriceStreak has earned:

| Streak | The chain |
|---|---|
| under a week | a bare cord |
| 7 days | first real links |
| 30 days | a linked chain |
| 60 days | FORGED — the streak's activation mark |
| 100 days | HEAVY — links thicken |
| 365 days | **GOLD** — the whole chain and ring recast |
| 1,000 days | **CHROME** — the monument |

One missed day resets a PriceStreak — that has always been the rule, and the chain tells it honestly. There is no way to buy a longer chain. There is only tomorrow.

## The finish is your rank

PriceRank recasts the toy itself: tier 3 lays a gloss over the plastic, tier 5 seeds it with glitter, tier 7 pours it in gold, tier 9 in liquid chrome. Rank comes only from un-fakeable onchain truth — so a chrome charm is a résumé you can dangle.

## Polish — the sync ritual

Your streak and rank live on PD; your charm lives onchain. **POLISH** joins them: the app signs a sealed attestation of your current numbers, you submit it with a tap, and the charm redraws itself. You pay pennies of gas; the platform pays nothing. Polish whenever you like — after a milestone, before a flex, or just to keep the shine honest. Each attestation is single-use and expires quickly, and the contract rejects any tampered claim: your numbers are exactly what the app signed, or nothing.

## The bond

Shine cannot be bought, inherited, or transferred — only lived. Every hand-to-hand transfer wipes the polish back to bare cord and base finish. The character, palette, accessory, and name survive; the life does not. This is what makes a worn charm on someone's profile mean something: it is proof of days, in an economy where days can't be faked.

## Christening

Once — and only once, ever — a keeper may name their charm: 2 to 12 characters, capitals, numbers and spaces. The name is engraved on a white tag that hangs off the keyring, and it stays with the charm through every future keeper, forever. Choose like it's permanent, because it is.

## Wearing it

Equip one of your Keychains and it appears as a **mini charm at the end of your profile tags** — the only place on PD your streak and rank exist as an object other people can see. Nothing is worn by default; the flex is a choice. Tap anyone's worn charm to see the full keychain, chain and all.

## Keychains and the machine's honesty

The charm's genes are frozen at the crank; the chain and finish are attested by the app and verified by the contract; the art is drawn by the contract itself, forever, with no server in the loop. If PD's lights went out tomorrow, every Keychain would keep rendering, keep swinging, and keep blinking — carrying the last polished day of its keeper's life, onchain, indefinitely.
