## The Binder

**MY BINDER** (the cap on the Sticker Exchange's crawl line) is the completionist view: every sheet is a binder page of slots. Stickers you hold print full-strength in their slots; stickers you're missing sit as dimmed outlines, each one a named gap. Every page carries its tally and a progress bar; a finished page stamps **COMPLETE ✓**; the top of the Binder totals the whole collection.

The Binder is built to be read next to [the Marketplace](/docs/stickers/the-marketplace): the outlines are your shopping list, the singles market is where they're filled, and **✛ WANT THIS** turns a gap into a standing signal.

## Your profile hero

Active stickers decorate the top of your profile — the **hero** — and every visitor sees them. The hero starts **generative**: the app arranges your active stickers for you, differently on each shuffle, always presentable. What shows and how it's arranged is entirely yours to control, and that control lives in the **Sticker Manager**: tap your own stickers on your own profile to open it.

## The Sticker Manager

The Manager is a compact popup pinned directly below your stickers, so you watch every change land live. Its pages (swipe, or tap the dots):

- **Layout** — the arrangement family, plus ⟳ **Shuffle** for a fresh roll of the current one. **Rows** chunks any layout into 1–3 rows.
- **Density · Align · Tilt** — how tightly stickers pile, which edge they gather to, and how jaunty they sit.
- **Flip · Border** — the upside-down option, and the kiss-cut edge treatment (border changes are the one control that never re-rolls your arrangement). Every arrangement always fits the screen — nothing ever hangs off the edge.
- **Sheets & Stickers** — the on/off layer. Whole sheets toggle with one pill; individual stickers toggle in the grid. Three rules keep it sane: your **first sheet turns itself on**, every **later purchase arrives off** (a new buy never clutters an arranged profile), and **at least one owned sheet always stays on**.

The sticker grid carries a **color filter** for big collections: tap a swatch for one color, long-press it for the whole hue family, or use the preset filters — **Match** (stickers that suit your current profile colorway), RGB, CMYK, and the painter's primaries.

**Manager Plus** (the ↑ in the header) expands to a full-screen version: a live preview up top — each opening plays a fresh entrance build — with every control on one page and the whole collection grid below.

## Hand-placing — the locked composition

Generative is the default; **dragging is the override.** The moment you drag any sticker on your own hero, the entire current picture freezes into a **locked composition**: every sticker exactly where and how it sits. From there you're composing by hand — drag to move, last-touched rides on top, ✕ prunes a sticker from the picture. While a composition is locked, **what you see is exactly what every visitor sees**, on any screen size.

Any layout control or Shuffle releases the lock and returns the hero to generative. Locked or generative, your arrangement follows your account across devices.

## Sticker Setup Codes

The Manager's footer carries a **Sticker Setup Code** — a short code encoding your entire current look, down to the exact generative roll. Copy it (⧉) to share an arrangement; paste any code and press Enter to wear it. **Surprise** rolls a completely random look when you'd rather be dealt one. Collectors trade these codes like the stickers themselves — and the code field is rumoured to understand a word or two that aren't codes at all.

## Hiding the layer

The **Sticker Mode** pill (⊞) in [MY PD](/docs/app/settings-and-display) hides your stickers when you want the quiet profile — the collection stays intact, arrangement and all, for whenever you flip it back.
