# Stickers — The Store

The Store is the primary market: every sheet PD has published, on one shelf. It opens as the Sticker Exchange's default face — a stacked grid of sheet cards you scroll (the ↑/↓ control in the header switches to a compact sideways rail), with a live crawl of store news above it and your running stats in the header: sheets available, stickers owned, and ◊ total spent.

**How:** Tap ⊞ STICKERS on the home action row — the Store is the Exchange's default face.

## Reading a sheet card

Each card fans three real stickers from the sheet — spaced across its range, so the fan is an honest preview, not a highlight reel — plus:

- **The name and rarity tag** — COMMON, UNCOMMON, RARE, or MYTHIC. The tag prices the sheet's ambition, not just its scarcity.
- **The sticker count** — how many stickers the sheet delivers.
- **The price and BUY SHEET** — the live ETH price. Prices are tuned to stay pocket-money (the platform targets roughly $22 a sheet and adjusts as ETH drifts).
- **LIVE / RESTOCK / ✓** — sheets on sale wear LIVE; a sheet scheduled to return wears a RESTOCK countdown in days; a ✓ marks sheets you already own.

Tap a card and the sheet itself opens: a die-cut paper of every sticker inside, re-scattered fresh each time you look. Different sheets lay out differently — logo sheets in a tidy grid, sprite and name sheets as a wrapping flow, the OUTPUTS sheet as a masonry of small artworks.

## Buying

One tap on **BUY SHEET** opens the confirm card — the same centered confirmation every money action on PD uses — with the sheet name, sticker count, and exact ETH price. Confirm, and the in-button progress bar fills while the purchase lands; the button settles on **OWNED** and every sticker in the sheet is yours. Buying requires a connected wallet, exactly like minting.

Two details worth knowing:

- **Your first sheet turns itself on; every later sheet arrives off.** New stickers never auto-clutter a profile arrangement you've already made — you opt each new sheet in from the [Sticker Manager](/docs/stickers/the-binder-and-your-profile).
- **What you buy is a sealed sheet.** The stickers are inside; getting them out is the peel.

## The peel

A sealed sheet you own wears its wrapper over the sticker paper: the sheet name, **SEALED · drag to peel**, and a peel tab. Put a finger (or pointer) on it and **drag** — the wrapper lifts, rotates, and past the point of no return it tears off for good, revealing the stickers underneath. The peel is deliberately a physical act, never a button, and it's recorded: each sheet's **sealed percentage** — how much of everything ever sold is still in its wrapper — becomes public market data on [the Marketplace](/docs/stickers/the-marketplace).

## Print runs and restocks

Sheets are print runs. A sheet can carry a hard cap and **sell out** — once the last copy sells, the primary door closes and the only way in is the Marketplace. Sold-out and pulled sheets can return in **restock windows** (the store counts down to them), and a restock can add new stickers to a sheet's pool. Two mechanics govern what a sheet delivers, both enforced by [the contract](/docs/contracts/pd-stickers):

- **FIXED sheets** — the launch model: every sheet delivers one of every sticker in it. Whole sheet, collect all. Supply is reserved the moment you buy, so a sealed FIXED sheet can always be peeled.
- **PACK sheets** — the shop's pack mode, available for future drops: peeling draws a fixed number of *distinct* stickers from a larger pool, rolled at the moment you peel with the same entropy recipe Project mints use. Capped rarities drop out of the pool as they exhaust — supply *is* the rarity — while an open-edition floor guarantees every sealed pack ever sold stays peelable.
