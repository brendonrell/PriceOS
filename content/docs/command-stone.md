# The Command Stone ⌘

The fastest path to anything in PD is not a menu. Signed in, **triple-tap the background of any page** — anywhere in the app, docs included — and the Command Stone rises: the platform's command line, in a speech bubble, open and ready to type.

Every other character on the platform has a personality. The Stone has **power and intelligence**. It reads what you meant, answers from the same ledger the rest of the platform runs on, and acts on the answer — all in one field.

## How it speaks

Every response arrives in the same speech bubble, built the same way, top to bottom:

1. **The dialogue** — two rows at the top. The Stone speaks first: a terse line, then a drier aside. Same question, same mood; new question, new mood.
2. **The widgets** — the middle of the bubble. Glanceable black cards carrying the actual answer: big type, real data, live numbers. Long answers scroll here while the dialogue and the button hold their ground.
3. **The action** — one button at the bottom. Every answer that can be acted on offers its one obvious next move: anchor the floor, wishlist the piece, open the winner, etch the to-do. One tap, done.

## What it understands

One input, no modes. The Stone hears which of these you meant:

- **Find** — the real global search, inline: people, projects, pieces, pages. Enter takes the top hit.
- **Ask** — questions about the market, answered from the ledger: floors, all-time highs, volumes, who holds what, first mints, release dates, the platform's biggest spenders.
- **Compute** — math typed straight in (`3*67`), money converted at the live rate (`0.05 eth to cad`), dates resolved from plain words (`next week tuesday`).
- **Etch** — natural-language creation: type a to-do, a note, an anchor, a watch or a wishlist line and commit it with a second touch. An etched item rides the same stores the full tools use.
- **Cast** — spell, mode and workspace names flip the real settings, by their exact names. Typing `fog` casts Fog.
- **Summon** — whole surfaces called by name and returned inline: `calendar`, `priceday`, `calc`, `gallery`, `matrix`, and the rest of the deck.

The **complete command reference** — every phrase, every widget, every example — lives on its own page: **[Abilities](/docs/command-stone/abilities)**.

## It remembers, and it sees

The Stone keeps its last subject. Ask *"prisms floor"*, then just *"ath"* — it knows you still mean Prisms. Summon it while looking at a piece and it adopts that piece as its subject, so a bare *"floor"* or *"calc 0.2"* means the thing on your screen.

## Getting rid of it

**Minimize** it to a single dot in the corner — swipe the bar down, or type `minimize` — and tap the dot to bring it back. **Close** it completely with another triple-tap on the background, or by typing `close`. It says goodbye. It means "see you soon."
