# The Command Stone ⌘

The fastest path to anything in PD is not a menu. Signed in, **triple-tap the background of any page** — anywhere in the app, docs included — and the Command Stone rises: the platform's command line, in a speech bubble, open and ready to type.

It is one field that answers in five different ways, and it works out which one you meant.

## Find anything

The real global search, inline: people, projects, pieces, pages. Results come as you type and Enter takes the top hit. If you know the name of the thing you want, this is always the shortest route to it.

## Ask it

Type a question about the market and it answers from the same ledger the rest of the platform runs on — floors, all-time highs, volumes, and answers drawn from these docs.

Answers arrive as glanceable black cards, and the cards **act**: anchor a floor, wishlist a piece, follow a collector, straight from the answer. It also remembers the subject you're on — ask *"prisms floor"*, then just *"ath"*.

## Etch

Type a to-do, a note, an anchor, a watch or a wishlist line and commit it with a second touch. Nothing is a scratchpad: an etched item rides the same stores the full tools use, so a to-do written here is the same to-do the [Sentinel](/docs/app/collector-tools) watches.

## Cast

Spell, mode and workspace names flip the real settings, by their exact names. Typing `fog` casts Fog. Typing `trader` wears the Trader workspace. The [Spell Book](/docs/app/spell-book) is the vocabulary.

## Summon widgets by name

Whole surfaces, called by name and returned inline: `calendar`, `priceday`, `calc`, `gallery`, `matrix`, `dossier @name`, `brief`, `wrapped`, and a project's `30d` trend sparkline, among others.

## Getting rid of it

**Minimize** it to a single dot in the corner — swipe the bar down, press and hold, or type `minimize` — and tap the dot to bring it back. **Close** it completely with another triple-tap on the background, or by typing `close`.
