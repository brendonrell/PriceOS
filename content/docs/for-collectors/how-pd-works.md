# For Collectors — How PD Works

Price Discussion is a place to collect curated generative art and to be part of the conversation the market has about it. This page is the plain-terms framing; [Wallet Setup](/docs/for-collectors/wallet-setup) and [The Secondary Market](/docs/for-collectors/the-secondary-market) go deeper.

## What you are collecting

An **Output** — one minted piece from an artist's Project. Every Output is an ERC-721 token whose artwork is generated by the artist's script from a hash assigned at mint, and both the script and the hash live on Ethereum itself. Your Output renders from chain data alone: no server, no pinned file, no platform dependency. Own the token, own the work — in the strongest sense the medium currently allows.

Every Project on PD passed curation, deployed its own immutable contract, and mints on fixed terms: fixed supply (22 to 9,999), fixed price, open to everyone. There are no allowlists at the contract level and no admin who can pause or change a live mint.

## Minting

When a Project is minting, its page carries the mint button. One tap prompts your wallet with the exact cost — mint price plus a small flat storage fee per Output, no surprises, no overpayment accepted. The moment the transaction confirms, your Output exists, its artwork is determined, and the artist has already been paid their 95% inside your very transaction.

## Trading

PD is not a marketplace. Secondary trading happens on third-party venues that honor Ethereum's royalty standard (EIP-2981 — a 5% royalty on every PD Project, 3% to the artist). PriceOS aggregates the resulting listings, offers, and sales into its own reading of the market: Price Story, the Tape, the Genome, Pings. On PD, watching prices move **is** the discussion.

## What's free

Everything that isn't a transaction: signing in, browsing, starring, wishlisting, following artists and collectors, building albums, setting price targets, and every tool in the app. You pay only when you mint or trade, and those costs are the chain's, visible in your wallet before you approve.

## What PD does not do

- **PD does not custody anything.** Your wallet holds your Outputs; PD has no access to your keys and no recovery flow that bypasses your wallet.
- **PD does not match buyers and sellers.** Secondary trades happen on third-party marketplaces.
- **PD cannot recover a lost seed phrase.** Nobody can — that is the deal with self-custody.
