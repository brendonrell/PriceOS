# Albums ◰

**Star it, want it, shelve it.** Starring is a bookmark and the Wishlist is buy
intent — an **Album** is the third thing: a set you built on purpose, in an
order you chose, meant to be looked at.

**◰** is the Albums mark across PD.

**How:** Tap ◰ on any artwork to open the Add-to-Album sheet; your albums live on your profile.

## Numbered, never named

An album has no title. It is **ALBUM 01**, **ALBUM 02**, in the order you made
them, and that number is its identity — "@collector's Album 03" is how the
whole platform refers to it.

That is deliberate. Albums are seen by other people, and PD gives users no
public-facing writing beyond their @name. What an album says, it says with the
art in it. (The opposite trade is [Lists](/docs/lists) — private, and named for
exactly that reason.)

There's no cap. Make as many as you want.

## Albums are public

**Anyone can see an album and who made it.** A tile carries the maker's @name
over the album number wherever it appears away from their own profile, so
curation is always attributable.

What that means in practice:

- Your albums are visible on your profile to visitors, not just to you.
- Every album holding a piece of a Project shows up on that Project's
  **+ More › Albums** tab — everyone's, not just yours — so a project page
  reads as *how the whole platform has shelved this work*.
- An artwork's own **Albums** tab shows the albums it sits in.
- Shelving an album surfaces it to your followers in the social feed.

The rest of your saving layer is unchanged and still private: Starred, the
Wishlist, Lists, and History are yours alone. See
[What's Private](/docs/whats-private).

## The cover

An album's tile is a **living mosaic** — the first four pieces, in a 2×2 grid,
quietly swapping one cell at a time for other members whenever the album holds
more than four. It never sits still, and it never shows you the same four
forever.

Pick a **COVER** instead and the tile becomes that single piece, full bleed, and
stops shuffling.

Under the art, the tile reads the album's **number**, its **count**, and — when
any member is listed — its **◊ worth**, the live sum of what those pieces are
asking right now.

## Getting pieces in

- **◰ on any artwork** opens the Add-to-Album sheet.
- **A multi-select bar** — tick pieces anywhere they're selectable and file the
  whole selection at once.
- **A Project's Albums tab** offers your pieces from that collection straight
  under the shelf: tap to pick, then **＋ ADD TO ALBUM**. Hold none of it yet
  and it offers the first four minted pieces so you can start a shelf anyway.

The sheet lists your albums with a tick on the ones already holding the piece,
plus **New Album** to make one and file in a single move.

## Inside an album

Tap any piece to open it.

**▶** plays the album **full screen** — one piece at a time, crossfading, the
whole shelf as an exhibition. (In [ASCII Art Mode](/docs/app/settings-and-display)
the show exhibits the text backups instead.)

**SELECT** turns on the power tools:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| REMOVE | Pulls the ticked pieces out. They stay yours, and stay starred. |
| MOVE | Sends them to another album — out of this one, into that one, one step. |
| FRONT | Jumps them to the front of the order. |
| COVER | Pins one ticked piece as the album's cover. |
| DELETE ALBUM | Deletes the shelf. Every piece in it stays exactly where it was. |

An album's order is **yours** — it never re-sorts itself behind your back.

## Albums and the Vault

[The Vault](/docs/app/identity-and-profiles) (⧈) works the same way, for pieces
you actually **own**: numbered walls of grails, with a stats block reading the
wall's real money and rarity. An album can hold anything on PD; a vault holds
only your own.
