# What's Private on PD

A short page, on purpose: most of PD is public, and [What's Public](/docs/whats-public) is the long list. What follows is everything only you ever see — and the platform's one hard promise about data it never collects in the first place.

## Your private workspace

The tools you think with are yours alone, always:

- **Notes** (⊟) — on pieces and on calendar days. Owner-only, forever. Write the real reason you bought it.
- **Stars** (★) — the silent piece bookmark. Private, and deliberately signal-free: starring a piece never generates a Ping to anyone.
- **Wishlist** (✛) — private buy intent. It drives *your* Pings (wishlist hits), never anyone else's view of you.
- **Cart** (▢) and **Bench** — session working state.
- **To-Dos** (❍), your **Calendar layer**, **Workflows** (☇), and every **Sentinel** target — including the ETH targets and budgets (◊) attached to them. The platform's watcher reads them to serve you; no other user can.
- **Grail Pins**, **grid presets**, **Starred rows** (artists, projects, traits, soundtracks, collectors) — your declared taste feeds your own interest Pings and stays your own.

## Your calls, sealed

**Price Targets** — your monthly floor call on a Project — are **sealed** while the window runs: only the count of calls shows. When the month turns, last window's crowd reveals as an anonymous histogram against where the floor actually landed. Your individual call is never published with your name, during or after.

## Your preferences

Display settings, colorway, workspaces, every display mode and spell, ping controls, and your muted list (the Hammer ᚦ) — all yours alone. Settings follow your account, not your browser, but they follow it privately.

## Studio, until you publish

Draft Projects in [PD Studio](/docs/studio/overview) — scripts, test runs, parameters — are visible only to the wallet (or device) that made them. Nothing about a draft touches the chain or the platform's public surfaces until you deploy. The same goes for the [submission](/docs/for-artists/submit-a-project) conversation itself.

## What PD never asks for

There is no personal-information layer to leak: **a wallet is the entire account.** No email, no name, no phone number, no KYC. The single exception is opt-in and single-purpose — subscribing to [the Dispatch Digest](/docs/dispatch) stores the email you give it for exactly that delivery, with one-tap unsubscribe.

## The line, in one sentence

**Public is what you do and display; private is what you save, set, and intend.** When a feature moves something across that line — the way [Albums](/docs/albums) moved from own-profile display to public shelves — the docs and the surface both say so.
