# What's Private on PD

> **Rough outline — first pass.** A short list, on purpose. Most of PD is public; these are the things only you ever see.

## Your private workspace (only you)
- **Notes** on pieces and on days.
- **Wishlists** and piece-level **Stars / bookmarks** — your saved-for-later.
- **Cart** and **Bench** working state.
- **To-Dos** and the personal Calendar layer.

## Your preferences
- Display settings, colorway, and every toggle.
- Muted accounts.

## Studio (until you publish)
- Unpublished projects and device-local drafts stay yours until you hit publish.

## What PD never asks for
- No email, no name, no personal info — a wallet is the whole account. (The Dispatch email digest is the one opt-in, and only if you want it.)
